# Contributing to GovOS

Thank you for your interest in GovOS.

GovOS is currently in the **pre-development / foundation phase**. The core architecture is being designed and the first modules are being specified. Full code contributions will open once the architecture is documented and the first module is ready.

## How to Contribute Now

### For Governments
If your government is exploring sovereign digital infrastructure, we want to hear from you. Contact us at [govos.tellsmarty.com](https://govos.tellsmarty.com) to start a conversation about adoption.

### For Funders
We are raising the Government Sovereign Adoption Fund (GSAF) — $10M to build and deploy GovOS in the first African country. Contact us at [govos.tellsmarty.com](https://govos.tellsmarty.com).

### For Technical Partners
If you work on MOSIP, Mojaloop, GovStack, or comparable DPI infrastructure, we'd like to discuss integration. Open an issue or contact us directly.

### For Developers
Watch this repository. Architecture documentation and the first module specifications will be published here. Once published, we will open issues for contributions.

## Code of Conduct

GovOS is a public-good initiative. All contributors are expected to act with integrity, respect, and a commitment to the public benefit of the communities GovOS serves.
